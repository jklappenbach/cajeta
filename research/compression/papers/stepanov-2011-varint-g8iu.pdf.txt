name: SIMD-Based Decoding of Posting Lists (varint-G8IU)
description: Fixes the data block at 8 bytes with one control byte for a variable int count, designed for pshufb SIMD decode; ~300% faster than scalar VByte. Predecessor that Stream VByte/Masked VByte beat (interleaved control byte limits throughput). Note: Group Varint (Dean/folly) is the 4-ints-per-block byte codec.
url: https://www.stepanovpapers.com/CIKM_2011.pdf
