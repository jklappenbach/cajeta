name: Zstandard Compression and the application/zstd Media Type (RFC 8878)
description: LZ77 dictionary stage + dual entropy backends (FSE/tANS for sequences, Huffman for literals) with multiple interleaved states for ILP/SIMD. Frame/block container: independent frames are the unit of zstd --threads (embarrassingly thread-parallel compress); overlapSize trades ratio for parallelism. Single-frame decode is serial but fast.
