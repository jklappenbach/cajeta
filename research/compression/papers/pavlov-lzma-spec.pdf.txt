name: LZMA / 7-Zip
description: LZ77 with very large dictionary (~4 GB) + repeated-distance optimization + a binary range coder driven by adaptive Markov context bit-probabilities. The MOST serial entropy design (each decoded bit updates the model for the next); high ratio, no SIMD; parallelism only via external block splitting.
